Nourishing Communities

In today’s climate of swelling inflation and widespread unemployment, growing fruit trees is more relevant than ever. It is an excellent source not only of nutritious food but also sustainable income for communities. Caring for them doesn’t have to be complicated—we outline the techniques to help you get the most out of these juicy treasure troves.

Practical Considerations

Light & Sunlight

Most trees require full sunlight for best yields, though various berries can thrive in shadier areas.

Climate & Frost

In frost-prone areas like South Africa's highveld, protect trees with burlap or cloth during cold winter snaps.

Soil & Water

Use permaculture designs like swales and berms to slow, spread, and sink water throughout your orchard.

High Density Orchard Culture (HDOC)

A method designed for high productivity without expensive commercial equipment. It moves away from traditional monoculture.

  • Higher Density: Plant more trees in a given area.
  • Size Management: Keep trees small and productive through pruning.
  • Biodiversity: Mix stone fruit, pome fruit, citrus, and nuts.
  • Succession Planting: Use early, mid, and late varieties for consistent harvests.
